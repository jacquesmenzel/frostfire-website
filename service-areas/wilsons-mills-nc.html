Short answer

We serve Wilson's Mills as part of our Triangle and Johnston County area. It's about 17 minutes (9 miles) from our base in Clayton without traffic, one of our shortest drives. We haven't completed a job here yet that our records show, so this page covers what's verifiably true about the town's homes. Call (919) 230-4439; Sunday is the same price.

More than half the homes here were built this decade

Johnston County records list 1,943 residential parcels with a Wilson's Mills mailing address. Their median build year is 2020, the middle 80% were built between 1990 and 2026, and 1,136 of them (58%) were built in the 2020s. There are 217 townhouses and 144 double-wide manufactured homes in the mix.

Census figures lag behind that building boom. The latest five-year estimate still puts the town's median build year at 1997 and shows 17% mobile homes, which describes Wilson's Mills before most of the new subdivisions went in.

Crescent Mills is one of those subdivisions, and it sits in Wilson's Mills, not Clayton, even though it's sometimes listed under Clayton. See our Crescent Mills page.

Almost every house runs on a heat pump

The county assessor lists 82% of Wilson's Mills homes with a heat pump and 16% with ducted forced air. Census estimates show 90% of occupied homes heating with electricity, 3% with utility gas and 3% with propane, even though Piedmont Natural Gas runs a lateral to the town.

What young heat pumps need is mostly unglamorous: a clear condensate drain, a correctly set thermostat (auxiliary heat shouldn't be running every morning), clean filters, and an outdoor unit that isn't buried in mulch or boxed in by new landscaping. In the nearby Clayton homes we service, the units we could date were a median of 5 years old, and the parts that failed most were small ones: capacitors, contactors and low-voltage wiring.

If your home is still under a builder's warranty, check that first. Either way, we're glad to diagnose it and give you a written finding.

Permits and power in Wilson's Mills

Town limits cover almost everything: 98% of the town's residential parcels are inside Wilson's Mills limits, so there's little of the town-versus-county confusion we see around Clayton.

Permits: Wilson's Mills has no inspections department. The town handles zoning and land use, then Johnston County Building Inspections (309 E. Market St, Smithfield; 919-989-5060) issues the mechanical permit. The county lists HVAC change-outs as permitted work.

Electric: not confirmed. We didn't find a town or utility page naming the provider. South River EMC, Tri-County EMC and Wake Electric all have territory somewhere in Johnston County, and third-party guides say Duke Energy Progress and/or South River EMC. Your bill is the reliable answer.

Gas: Piedmont Natural Gas.

Wilson's Mills questions

Is Crescent Mills in Wilson's Mills or Clayton?

Wilson's Mills. Developer, builder and county records place Crescent Mills in Wilson's Mills, Johnston County, even though some listings use a Clayton name.

Who issues HVAC permits in Wilson's Mills?

Johnston County Building Inspections, after the town's zoning and land-use step. Wilson's Mills does not run its own inspections department.

Which electric company serves Wilson's Mills?

We have not confirmed it. No town or utility page names the provider; third-party guides say Duke Energy Progress and/or South River EMC. Check your bill.
