Holly Springs calls are mostly about heat

56% of our Holly Springs jobs came between November and March. February and March were the busiest months, with 4 jobs each. Only 22% came in the June-to-September cooling season. "No heat" was the most common complaint by a wide margin (6 jobs).

That fits the town's heating mix. The Census estimates about 62% of occupied Holly Springs homes heat with natural gas and 37% with electricity. So the typical Holly Springs no-heat call is a gas furnace: an ignition or flame-sensing problem, an inducer motor, a pressure switch, or a control board. On the electric side it's a heat pump that isn't defrosting or a heat strip that isn't coming on.

A town built since the 1990s

Fewer than 800 of today's Holly Springs-address homes were built before 1990. Wake County records list 17,223 homes with a Holly Springs address; 3,262 were built in the 1990s, 5,287 in the 2000s, 4,394 in the 2010s and 3,531 since 2020. Only about 10% are older than 1995. The Census puts 79% of homes in town as single-family detached, one of the highest shares we see.

The homes we've worked in fit that pattern. The 15 matched to county records range from 1993 to 2022, with a median of 2013. Every brand we've recorded on a Holly Springs nameplate has been Lennox (6 units). The two we could date were made in 2020 and 2021.

Permits through CityView

The Town of Holly Springs Development Services office issues HVAC trade permits through its CityView portal. Contractors who aren't already in the town's Contractor Search have to register first. Inspection requests made before 4:00 p.m. go on the next business day's schedule, and trade finals have to pass before the building final. The town's homeowner guide says a trade permit covers installation, extension, relocation, replacement and general repair of any heating, air-conditioning or venting system. We've seen two different phone numbers listed for Development Services, so we haven't printed one here.

About 90% of Holly Springs-address homes are inside town jurisdiction. Nearly all of our Holly Springs jobs were, too (14 of 15 matched), with one under Fuquay-Varina.

Utilities

Holly Springs heating and HVAC FAQ

Do you do furnace repair in Holly Springs?

Yes. About 62% of Holly Springs homes heat with natural gas, so gas furnace repair is a big part of our work here. No-heat calls were our most common Holly Springs complaint.

Does Holly Springs require a permit for HVAC repair?

The town says an HVAC (Trade) Permit is required for the installation, extension, relocation, replacement and general repair of any heating, air-conditioning or venting system. Contractors file it through the town's CityView portal.

Why does one floor of my newer Holly Springs house never warm up?

In a zoned system, a failed damper or zone control can keep air from reaching one floor. We diagnosed zone control problems in two Holly Springs homes built in 2020 and 2022 this year.
