Furnace and AC Repair in Rolesville, NC

Unlike the Johnston County towns, Rolesville runs on gas heat, and its first big wave of systems is reaching 20 years old.

Short answer

We repair gas furnaces, heat pumps and central air in Rolesville as part of our Raleigh–Durham service area. It's one of our longer drives, about 39 minutes (23.8 miles) from Clayton without traffic, so we give you a real arrival window. Call (919) 230-4439 to book; Sundays cost the same.

Rolesville heats with gas

Census estimates show 68% of occupied Rolesville homes heating with utility gas, 27% with electricity and 4% with propane. That's close to the reverse of most Johnston County towns we serve, where heat pumps dominate. In Rolesville, a winter "no heat" call is most likely a gas furnace: ignition, flame sensing, the inducer motor, or a pressure switch that won't close.

Gas utility: Enbridge Gas North Carolina, (877) 776-2427. That's the company formerly known as PSNC; Dominion Energy sold it to Enbridge on October 1, 2024. Some older town pages still say PSNC or Dominion, with the same phone number.

The 2000s build-out is reaching 20 years

Wake County records list 3,991 residential parcels with a Rolesville address. 90% were built in 2000 or later, and the median is 2014. That includes 1,044 homes from the 2000s, 1,329 from the 2010s and 1,215 so far in the 2020s. The equipment that went into those 2000s homes is now 17 to 26 years old if it's original.

Our two Rolesville jobs show both ends of that:

  • October 2026, 2005-built home: a 2005 Carrier unit, about 21 years old, with a failed capacitor and a clogged condensate drain.
  • July 2025: an International Comfort Products unit with a 2013 manufacture date.

Between the two visits we also logged a refrigerant leak and a low-voltage wiring fault. When a system is past 20 years, we tell you what failed, what else we see wearing, and whether repair still makes sense, so you can decide.

Permits go through Wake County's portal

Rolesville splits the work: the Town of Rolesville Planning Department (919-554-6517) reviews zoning and land use, then Wake County Permits & Inspections reviews, approves and inspects. Applications go through the Wake County Permit Portal, and inspections are requested there or through Wake County Inspections at 919-856-6222.

Wake County's FAQ says a mechanical permit is required for installing, extending, relocating, replacing or doing general repair on any heating, air-conditioning or venting system. Nearly all Rolesville-address parcels (98.7%) are in the town's planning jurisdiction; the rest fall under Wake County directly.

Electric service is split East and West

The town says electric service in Rolesville is divided between two providers: Duke Energy Progress and Wake Electric Membership Corporation (919-863-6300). We haven't confirmed which side of town each serves, so check your bill.

Rolesville questions

Who is my electric provider in Rolesville?

Either Duke Energy Progress or Wake Electric. The town says service is split East and West between the two, and we have not confirmed the boundary, so check your bill.

My gas bill says Enbridge, not PSNC. Is that the same company?

Yes. Dominion Energy sold PSNC to Enbridge on October 1, 2024, and it now does business as Enbridge Gas North Carolina, with the same 877-776-2427 number.

Who inspects HVAC work in Rolesville?

Wake County Permits and Inspections, after the Town of Rolesville's zoning review. Permits and inspection requests go through the Wake County Permit Portal.

My system is from the mid-2000s. Should I repair it or replace it?

It depends on what failed and the condition of the rest of the system. On the 21-year-old Carrier we serviced in October 2026, the problems were a capacitor and a clogged drain. We show you what we find and let you decide.
