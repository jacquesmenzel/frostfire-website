Why so many Wendell calls come in May

Four of our eight Wendell jobs were in May. The first hot week is when a system that sat idle all winter gets asked to run hard, and the small parts go first: capacitors, contactors, drains. Even the three-year-old Trane we maintained in July 2026 had a failed capacitor and contactor, which is why a check before the first heat wave is worth it.

Wendell's housing is young, which fits. Wake County records list 11,526 residential parcels with a Wendell address, with a median build year of 2015. 4,417 of them (38%) were built in the 2020s. The homes we've worked in range from 1993 to 2022, median 2015. Census estimates for the town show half of homes built since 2010 and about 22% before 1980.

Heating is mixed: 60% of occupied homes heat with electricity, 34% with utility gas and 6% with propane.

Permits: town first, then Wake County, unless you're outside the town

For property inside Wendell's town limits or ETJ, permits start with the Town of Wendell Planning Department. Mechanical permits are then submitted, paid for and issued in the Wake County Permit Portal, and Wake County schedules all inspections. The town does its own zoning setback inspection first.

About 24% of Wendell-address parcels are in Wake County's own planning jurisdiction, not the town's. For those, Wake County handles the permit directly. Of our seven parcel-matched Wendell jobs, five were in the town's jurisdiction and two were in Wake County's.

Wendell utilities

Gas: the town page lists "Dominion Energy, (877) 776-2427." That's the old name. Dominion sold PSNC to Enbridge in October 2024, and it's now Enbridge Gas North Carolina, with the same number.
