Most Benson homes are outside town limits, and so are all of our jobs here

We've completed five Benson jobs between February 2025 and October 2026, four repairs and one estimate. Every one of them was in unincorporated Johnston County, not inside the Town of Benson. That isn't unusual: of the 6,717 residential parcels with a Benson mailing address, 70% are unincorporated, 18% are inside town limits and 12% are in the town's ETJ.

The homes we visited were built between 1993 and 2007. The county assessor lists three of them with ducted forced air and two with heat pumps. Three of the five calls came in February or March, and one was on a Saturday.

The housing stock is also more varied than the newer subdivisions closer to Raleigh. Besides 6,082 single-family homes, county records list 439 double-wide and 70 single-wide manufactured homes with Benson addresses. Manufactured homes use different air handlers and duct layouts from site-built houses, so mention it when you call and we'll bring the right parts.

How Benson homes are heated

  • Johnston County's assessor lists 72% of Benson-mailing homes with a heat pump, about three in four, and 23% with ducted forced air.
  • Census estimates for the town itself show 81% of occupied homes heating with electricity and 18% with utility gas.
  • Build years run from the 1800s to brand-new: the county median is 2002, 26% were built before 1980, and 1,918 homes (29%) were built in the 2020s.

With that many heat pumps, a cold-snap call is usually about auxiliary or emergency heat strips, defrost, or a heat pump that can't keep up. In older houses we check the ductwork too, not just the equipment.

Permits and utilities around Benson

Permits: Benson doesn't run its own inspections department. Johnston County Building Inspections (309 E. Market St, Smithfield; 919-989-5060) issues mechanical permits for both the town and the unincorporated land around it. Inside town limits, the owner first gets a zoning / land-use permit from the town. The county lists HVAC change-outs as permitted work and accepts a simple trade permit by email.

Electric: inside town, the Town of Benson Electric Department (303 E. Church St, 919-894-3553) is the public power provider. For rural Benson addresses outside the town system, we haven't confirmed the provider, so check your bill.

Gas: Piedmont Natural Gas runs a lateral to Benson off its main line across the county.

Not confirmed: some Benson mailing addresses may fall in Harnett County. We haven't confirmed which electric company or permit office covers those, so we check the parcel before quoting a replacement.

Benson-area questions

I live outside Benson town limits. Who issues my HVAC permit?

Johnston County Building Inspections. Benson relies on the county for building and trade permits, and every Benson job we have completed so far was outside town limits.

Who is my electric provider in Benson?

Inside town, it's the Town of Benson Electric Department. Rural Benson addresses outside the town system may have a different provider, which we have not confirmed, so check your bill. Natural gas in Benson comes from Piedmont Natural Gas.
