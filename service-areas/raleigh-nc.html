Permits: City of Raleigh or Wake County?

A Raleigh mailing address doesn't always mean a City of Raleigh permit. Of our Raleigh-address jobs that matched a county parcel, 197 were inside the City of Raleigh's planning jurisdiction and 15 were in unincorporated Wake County. A handful fell under Cary, Garner or Fuquay-Varina.

  • Inside the city: permits go through City of Raleigh Planning and Development and its Permit and Development Portal. A like-for-like HVAC replacement needs a permit but no plans. New equipment where none existed needs a plot plan or survey. The city's permit helpline is 919-996-2500.
  • Unincorporated Wake: the permit goes through Wake County Permits and Inspections.

Repairs like a capacitor or a drain line don't involve you in any of this. On a replacement, the licensed contractor pulls the permit and meets the inspector.
