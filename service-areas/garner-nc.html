Two generations of Garner houses

Garner's housing comes in two waves. Wake County records list 14,981 homes with a Garner mailing address. About 4,000 of them were built in the 1950s through the 1970s. Building held steady through the 1980s, 1990s and 2000s, then surged: 2,979 Garner homes date from the 2020s, more than any earlier decade. The Census estimates that about 29% of homes inside the town were built before 1980 and another 29% in 2010 or later.

The houses we've worked in cover the whole range. Of 23 Garner jobs matched to a county record, the oldest home was built in 1970 and the newest in 2022, with a median of 2006.

Those two waves need different things. A house from the 1960s has had its equipment replaced at least once, and the ductwork may be older than the system. A 2020s build is still on its original equipment, and its first problems tend to be small: a capacitor, a drain line, a defrost control.

Heat pumps are the norm here

The Census estimates that 60% of occupied Garner homes heat with electricity, mostly heat pumps, and 36% with natural gas. About 3% use propane, more than in the towns north and west of Raleigh. Half of our Garner work came in winter: 48% of jobs fell between November and March, with January the busiest month (7 jobs). July was just as busy, with 7 more.

Garner FAQ

Who issues the HVAC permit for a Garner address?

It depends on where the house sits, not the mailing address. Inside town limits, the Town of Garner Inspections Department issues it through the SmartGov portal. Unincorporated Wake County addresses with a Garner mailing address go through Wake County Permits and Inspections. Johnston County homes in the Cleveland area go through Johnston County Building Inspections.

How fast can you get to Garner?

Garner is about 23 minutes and 12 miles from our shop in Clayton with no traffic, which makes it one of the shortest drives we make in Wake County.

My Garner heat pump is blowing cool air in winter. What's wrong?

Common causes are a defrost problem, a stuck reversing valve, low refrigerant or a failed heat strip. We've diagnosed reversing valve and defrost faults on Garner jobs. Have it checked before the electric bill jumps.

Can I pull my own mechanical permit in Garner?

The Town of Garner offers an Owner Mechanical Permit Affidavit for homeowners doing their own mechanical work. If we do the work, we pull the permit.
