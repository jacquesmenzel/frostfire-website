A town of townhomes

Morrisville's housing is unlike the rest of the towns we serve. By Census estimates, only about 33% of homes here are single-family detached houses. The rest are townhomes, condos and apartments. Most of it went up in a short window: of 8,883 homes with a Morrisville address in Wake County records, 4,511 were built in the 2000s and 2,525 in the 2010s. The homes we've serviced here have a median build year of 2015.

Multi-story attached homes often run one system with zone dampers, or a separate system per floor.

Zoning problems show up a lot here

Zone damper and zone control faults came up on 5 of our 29 Morrisville jobs. That's about one job in six, a higher share than we see in Raleigh, Durham or Cary. The symptom is easy to recognize: the thermostat on one floor is satisfied while another floor never catches up. Often the fix is a damper actuator or a wiring repair, not a new system.

Permits in Morrisville

The Town of Morrisville Inspections Department handles mechanical permits. Its rules are stricter than some neighbors': a permit is required for the installation, extension, relocation, replacement and general repair of any heating, air-conditioning or venting system. Mechanical contractors sign the town's Contractor Signature Form. Applications, payment and inspection scheduling all happen online; the town doesn't take inspection requests by phone, and requests in before 2 p.m. get the next business day. The office is at 919-463-6190.

A small part of Morrisville is in Durham County. We found no separate Durham process, but we haven't confirmed that the town handles those addresses too.

Morrisville HVAC questions

Why is one floor of my Morrisville townhome comfortable and the other isn't?

In a zoned system, motorized dampers in the ductwork send air to each floor. When a damper actuator fails or its wiring faults, one zone gets too much air and the other too little. Zone damper or zone control problems showed up on 5 of our 29 Morrisville jobs.

Does Morrisville require a permit for HVAC repair?

The Town of Morrisville requires a permit for the installation, extension, relocation, replacement and general repair of any heating, air-conditioning or venting system. The contractor applies through the town's online portal.

Who are the utilities in Morrisville?

The town lists Duke Energy Progress for electricity and Enbridge Gas North Carolina (formerly PSNC) for natural gas.
