An electric-heat town

The Census estimates 58% of occupied Knightdale homes heat with electricity and 39% with natural gas. In practice, many of those electric homes have a heat pump with electric heat strips as backup. A heat pump moves heat instead of making it, which is efficient until it can't keep up. Then the strips switch on, and the thermostat shows "aux" or "emergency" heat.

Those strips run on their own breakers and relays. When one fails, the house can go cold on the first hard freeze, even though the heat pump seems to be running. When the heat pump fails and the strips do all the work, the house stays warm but the bill climbs.

The Knightdale permit setup

Knightdale splits the work. The Town of Knightdale Development Services issues trade permits for HVAC, electrical, plumbing and refrigeration, but the application is submitted through the Wake County Permit Portal, and Wake County Permits and Inspections does the mechanical inspections under contract with the town. Fees go to Wake County, by portal or by check. Next-day inspection requests have to be in by 3:00 p.m. the business day before.

Wake County's rule is that a mechanical permit is needed for the installation, extension, relocation, replacement and general repair of any heating, air-conditioning or venting system. The contractor has to hold a portal account and be linked to the permit, so on a replacement we handle it.

Not every Knightdale address is in town jurisdiction. About 20% of Knightdale-address homes are in unincorporated Wake County, and 2 of our 11 jobs were there. Those go straight to Wake County.

Knightdale heat pump and HVAC FAQ

Why does my Knightdale thermostat say 'aux heat' all the time?

Aux heat means the electric heat strips are helping the heat pump. A little of that on a cold morning is normal. Running on aux constantly usually means the heat pump itself isn't carrying the load, from low refrigerant, a defrost problem or a failed outdoor part, and it costs a lot more to run. We've worked on heat strip and auxiliary heat problems in Knightdale homes.

Who handles HVAC permits in Knightdale?

The Town of Knightdale issues trade permits, but the application goes through the Wake County Permit Portal and Wake County does the mechanical inspections for the town under contract. Fees are paid to Wake County.

Is Knightdale on Duke Energy or Wake Electric?

Both serve the area; the town lists Duke Energy Progress and Wake Electric Membership Corporation. We haven't confirmed the boundary, so check your bill.
