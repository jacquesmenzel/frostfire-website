Rooftop Unit Problems We Diagnose & Repair

RTU Not Cooling

Low airflow, dirty coils, refrigerant issues, failed compressors, or condenser fan failures that leave the space hot.

Heating / Ignition Failures

Gas heat ignition problems, limit trips, and heat exchanger concerns that leave offices and dining rooms cold.

Electrical & Control Board Issues

Tripped breakers, bad contactors, failed capacitors, and control boards that keep the unit from starting.

Blower & Airflow Problems

Weak supply air, clogged filters, belt issues, and economizer dampers stuck open or closed.

Restaurant Kitchen Load Stress

RTUs fighting kitchen heat, hood make-up air imbalance, and systems that can't keep up during peak service.

After-Hours Commercial Emergencies

Nights and weekends — we respond when a failed RTU would otherwise force you to close early.
