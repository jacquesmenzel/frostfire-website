Common Walk-In Cooler Failures We Fix

Compressor & Condensing Unit Issues

Short cycling, hard starts, overheating, or a unit that runs constantly without pulling down temperature.

Evaporator Coil & Fan Problems

Iced coils, weak airflow, noisy evaporator fans, and uneven temperatures from front to back of the box.

Refrigerant Leaks

Low charge, oil stains, hissing, or gradual warm-up. We locate leaks, repair, and recharge to manufacturer specs.

Door Seals, Latches & Heaters

Torn gaskets, doors that won't seal, and failed door heaters that cause ice buildup and energy waste.

Controls & Thermostats

Failed controllers, bad sensors, and defrost timers that leave the box too warm or locked in defrost.

Emergency After-Hours Failures

Nights, weekends, and holidays — we treat cooler emergencies as priority commercial calls.
