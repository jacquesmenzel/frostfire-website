Why This Direction

Make it feel premium, not playful.

The best-performing quote flows I reviewed all keep the first screen simple: show credible options, explain what happens next, and avoid anything that feels like a toy.

03
Book the in-home estimate
The calculator should qualify interest and move them into a real appointment, not pretend to replace one.

Design Notes

What feels more professional here

  • Large, clean cards with real service language instead of emoji-heavy quiz tiles.
  • The right rail keeps business value visible without hijacking the main decision.
  • The first screen is framed like a consultation tool, not a game.
