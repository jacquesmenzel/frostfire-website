Short answer

We service heat pumps, AC and furnaces in Shotwell, the rural community in unincorporated Wake County, and in the homes along Shotwell Road in Clayton. Shotwell sits about 6 miles north of Clayton, so it's a short drive for us; call (919) 230-4439 for today's window. Same price on Sundays.

Which Shotwell are you in?

Shotwell, the community, is a historic settlement in unincorporated eastern Wake County, about 4 miles south of Knightdale and about 6 miles north of Clayton. People have lived there since the early 1800s; the 1818 Oaky Grove house is on the National Register. It is not part of Clayton, and it isn't in Johnston County.

Shotwell Road runs into Clayton in Johnston County, meeting US 70 (now Clayton Boulevard). Clayton annexed several subdivisions along it: Shotwell Station in 2002, Magnolia Pointe in 2003, LionsGate Village in 2005, plus a water tower site, according to the town's annexation history.

This matters most for one thing: who issues your HVAC permit.

Shotwell in Wake County: Wake permits

In unincorporated Wake County, HVAC work is permitted by Wake County Permit and Inspections. A replacement that doesn't need a building permit uses a trade permit through Wake's online portal. The application asks for the appliance type, whether it's gas or electric, and where it sits: outside, in the crawlspace, attic or basement. When you call us about a Wake-side Shotwell home, those are useful details to have ready, and they're the same ones that tell us what parts to bring.

Shotwell Road in Johnston County: Clayton permits

The Shotwell Road homes we matched in Johnston County records (45 of them) are all in Clayton's extraterritorial jurisdiction. In the ETJ, as in town limits, the Town of Clayton handles HVAC permits, not the county. Clayton requires a mechanical permit to install, replace, relocate or do general repair on heating and air systems; see its inspections FAQ.

Shotwell questions

Is Shotwell part of Clayton?

The historic Shotwell community is not. It's unincorporated Wake County, north of Clayton. Shotwell Road in Johnston County runs into Clayton, and parts of it are in Clayton's town limits or ETJ.

Who issues HVAC permits in Shotwell?

Wake County Permit and Inspections for the Wake County community. For Shotwell Road homes in Clayton's ETJ or town limits, the Town of Clayton.

What will Wake County ask for on an HVAC trade permit?

The appliance type, gas or electric, and where the equipment sits: exterior, crawlspace, attic or basement. We file it.
