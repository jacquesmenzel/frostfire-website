The first replacement decision

For the early South Lakes homes, the first replacement is the conversation that comes up. A few things to know before you get quotes:

  • Refrigerant changed. Systems built from 2025 on use A2L refrigerants (R-454B or R-32) instead of the R-410A in most South Lakes homes. Replacing only the outdoor unit and keeping an old indoor coil is generally not an option with the new refrigerants. Plan on a matched system.
  • Size it to the house, not the old label. Builder systems are sometimes sized by rule of thumb. A replacement should be sized from a load calculation, not by copying the old tonnage, so ask anyone quoting you whether they ran one.
  • Repair first when it makes sense. A 2014 heat pump with a failed capacitor needs a capacitor, not a new system. We will tell you which side of that line you are on.

South Lakes FAQ

My South Lakes home was built around 2008. Is it time to replace the HVAC?

If the original system is still in place, it is 17 to 19 years old, so it is worth planning a replacement rather than waiting for a failure. Repair still makes sense for a single failed part.

Can I replace just the outdoor unit?

Usually not anymore. New systems use A2L refrigerants such as R-454B or R-32, so the indoor coil and outdoor unit should be replaced as a matched system.

Who permits HVAC work in South Lakes?

The Town of Fuquay-Varina Inspections Department, through its E-Permits portal. All South Lakes parcels are inside town limits.
