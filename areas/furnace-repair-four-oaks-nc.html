Electric furnace repairs

An electric furnace has no burner. It has heating elements, sequencers or relays that switch them on in stages, limit switches, and a blower. Common calls:

  • Blower runs, air is lukewarm: one or more elements or sequencers have failed, so only part of the heat comes on.
  • Breaker trips when heat starts: a shorted element or a failed breaker. Reset it once. If it trips again, leave it off and call.
  • Burning-dust smell on the first cold night: usually normal for a few minutes. A hot plastic or electrical smell is not; shut it off.

The same parts are the backup heat in a heat pump air handler, which is most of the homes in the first row of that table. If your heat pump is on "aux" all the time, read emergency heat vs. aux heat.

Propane and gas furnace repairs

A fuel-burning furnace has more ways to stop: the igniter, flame sensor, pressure switch, inducer motor, gas valve and venting all have to work in order. The control board usually flashes a code that tells us where it stopped. Our guides on a furnace blinking 4 times and a furnace that turns on then off explain the most common ones.

A propane furnace needs the right conversion for LP gas and a supply your propane company keeps full. Before any burner repair we check that the tank has fuel and the supply valve is open. An empty tank causes a surprising number of propane no-heat calls. If you ever smell gas, leave the house and call your propane supplier or 911 first. For carbon monoxide symptoms, see our CO and gas furnace guide.

Older site-built homes. About 1,740 Four Oaks-area houses were built before 1980, many in the 1930s–1950s. If you're tired of heating one room, we can price ducted options.

Manufactured homes. The county lists 543 double-wides and 68 single-wides with Four Oaks addresses. These usually have a downflow furnace in a closet, blowing into a duct under the floor. A replacement furnace has to be one listed for manufactured homes, and a crushed or disconnected floor duct can look like a furnace failure. We check both.

Furnace questions from the Four Oaks area

My furnace runs but blows cool air. Is it broken?

On an electric furnace, cool air with the blower running usually means one or more heating elements, sequencers or limit switches have failed. On a propane or gas furnace, it often means the burners never lit or shut off on a safety. Turn the thermostat down and call; don't keep resetting it.

Can you fix a furnace in a double-wide?

Yes. Manufactured-home furnaces are a different design from site-built units, and any replacement must be listed for manufactured-home use. About 1 in 10 Four Oaks-area parcels is a manufactured home.

Do I need a permit to replace a furnace in Four Oaks?

Yes. Inside town limits the Town of Four Oaks issues the mechanical permit; outside town jurisdiction, Johnston County Building Inspections does. We pull it as part of a replacement.

Do you work on propane furnaces?

Yes. Propane heats roughly 5% of homes in town and about 8% across the 27524 ZIP (Census estimates). Your propane supplier handles the tank and supply line; we handle the furnace.
