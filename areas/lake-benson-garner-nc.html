First, Lake Benson is a lake

People search "Lake Benson HVAC," so we answer it, but there is no Lake Benson subdivision. The lake is the City of Raleigh's secondary drinking-water source, treated at the Dempsey E. Benton Water Treatment Plant. The county parcel map does not show a single property with "Lake Benson" as its subdivision name. What exists are neighborhoods and rural lots around the shoreline and along the roads that carry the Benson name, and those properties are not all governed the same way.

We have not completed a job we could match to a street right by the lake yet. We work across Garner, and the lake area is on our normal route.

Why your address decides who inspects your new system

County records for Benson-named roads in ZIP 27529 show three kinds of parcels mixed together:

  1. Inside Garner town limits. The Town of Garner Inspections Department issues the mechanical permit through its SmartGov portal (paper applications are no longer taken).
  2. Garner's ETJ (extraterritorial jurisdiction). There is no city on the tax record, but the planning jurisdiction is still listed as Garner, so Garner handles permits.
  3. Unincorporated Wake County. Planning jurisdiction "WC." Here Wake County Permits and Inspections issues the permit, not Garner.

Two neighbors on the same road can land in different columns. Wake County's own advice is to look up the property in iMAPS and read the planning jurisdiction field. We do that before we quote a replacement, because the permit office changes the paperwork and the inspection scheduling, and a system swapped without a permit can cause trouble when you sell the house. If you are doing your own mechanical work inside Garner's jurisdiction, the town offers an Owner Mechanical Permit Affidavit.

What the housing around here looks like

We could not confirm a build era for the homes right around the lake, so here is the ZIP-wide picture from the Census Bureau's American Community Survey for 27529 (2024 five-year estimates):

  • Median year built: 2001. About 22% of homes were built before 1980, and about 29% in 2010 or later.
  • About 70% are single-family detached houses, and about 6% are mobile homes.
  • Heating fuel: about 70% electricity (heat pumps and electric furnaces), 25% utility gas, 4% propane.

That spread is wider than in Garner's newer subdivisions. In this ZIP, a 1970s house on a gas furnace with add-on AC can sit a mile from a 2010s all-electric heat pump home. The questions we ask on the phone change with it: what fuel, how old, where the indoor unit sits. If you have propane, tell us when you call, since the first check on a propane furnace is whether the tank and regulator are actually delivering gas.

Utilities

Garner's town directory lists Duke Energy for electricity. Gas is Enbridge Gas North Carolina, which older town pages still call PSNC; the name changed when Enbridge bought PSNC from Dominion in October 2024. Whether gas mains reach your road near the lake is not confirmed, so ask your neighbors or Enbridge before planning a gas furnace.

Lake Benson area FAQ

Is there a Lake Benson neighborhood?

No. Lake Benson is a City of Raleigh reservoir in Garner. The homes around it belong to various subdivisions and rural lots, with no subdivision named Lake Benson on county records.

Does Garner or Wake County permit HVAC work near Lake Benson?

It depends on the parcel. Properties in Garner town limits or Garner's ETJ go through the Town of Garner; unincorporated parcels marked WC go through Wake County Permits and Inspections. Check the planning jurisdiction in Wake County iMAPS, or we will check it for you.

How far are you from Lake Benson?

Garner is about 23 minutes and 12 miles from our Clayton base without traffic.

Do you work on propane furnaces?

Yes. About 4% of homes in ZIP 27529 heat with propane, according to Census estimates. Mention propane when you call so we bring the right parts.
