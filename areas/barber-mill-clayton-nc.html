Barber Mill is a road, not one neighborhood

People search for "Barber Mill" as if it were a subdivision. It's really a road corridor with several subdivisions, a townhome community and a lot of individual parcels along it. The Town of Clayton has annexed pieces of it over 20 years: the 34.7-acre Glen Haven subdivision (Section II in 2001, Phase 3 in 2006, Phase 4 in 2018), Hunter Way, a lot in Archdale in 2004, 222 Barber Mill Road in 2008, and a tract near Grovewood in 2005, according to the town's annexation history. Other stretches were never annexed.

Johnston County lists HVAC change-outs among the projects that need a permit. We look up your parcel and file with the right office; you don't have to figure it out.

Four kinds of houses on one road

The county's build years show why calls from Barber Mill Road vary so much. The median is 1988, but the spread is wide:

  • The 1990s subdivisions (65 homes) are the largest group, with 32 more from the 1980s. If original, those systems are 25 to 35 years old. Many have been replaced at least once.
  • Townhomes (55 parcels), including the Barber Mill Townhomes community on Cheltenham Drive. Townhome condensers often sit close together, which affects access and clearance when we replace one.
  • Manufactured homes (19), 18 double-wide and one single-wide. These use different air handlers, duct layouts and crossover connections than site-built houses.
  • Old farmhouses: 15 homes with county build years in the 1900s decade, and more from the 1940s through 1970s. Old houses usually mean retrofitted ducts in tight crawlspaces or attics.
  • Newer homes: 18 from the 2000s and 8 since 2010.

The assessor lists 57% of these homes with heat pumps and 40% with ducted forced air (usually a gas furnace). A handful show baseboard or non-ducted heat.

Electric and gas

The Town of Clayton has run its own public power system since 1913 and serves most customers inside town limits; Duke Energy Progress serves some areas. On a road that's part town, part ETJ and part county, we haven't confirmed the electric provider address by address. Piedmont Natural Gas serves the Clayton area, but whether a gas main runs along your stretch of Barber Mill Road isn't confirmed. Your bills will tell you.

Barber Mill Road questions

Is Barber Mill Road inside Clayton town limits?

Parts of it. County records put about 32% of homes inside town limits, 43% in Clayton's ETJ and 26% in unincorporated Johnston County.

Who issues the HVAC permit for my Barber Mill Road home?

The Town of Clayton if you're in town limits or the ETJ; Johnston County Building Inspections if you're outside both. We check your parcel and file with the right office.

Do you work on manufactured homes on Barber Mill Road?

Yes. About 19 homes on the road are manufactured homes, and we service their heat pumps, AC and air handlers.

Is it more expensive to get service on a Sunday?

No. Sundays cost the same as weekdays.
