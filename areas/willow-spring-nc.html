HVAC repair in Willow Spring, NC

An unincorporated community in the southern Triangle, split between Wake and Johnston counties.

Short answer

Wake side or Johnston side? It changes the permit

Willow Spring is not a town, and a Willow Spring mailing address does not tell you who regulates your house. Of the 7,152 residential parcels with a Willow Spring postal city, 4,144 are in Wake County and 3,008 are in Johnston County.

  • Wake County side. About 83% of these parcels are in Wake County's own planning jurisdiction, so Wake County Permits and Inspections issues the mechanical permit. Wake requires one for installing, replacing or doing general repair on any heating, air-conditioning or venting system. About 14% fall in Fuquay-Varina's jurisdiction and 3% in Angier's, and those go through that town.
  • Johnston County side. Every Johnston parcel with a Willow Spring address is outside town limits, so Johnston County Building Inspections in Smithfield handles it. The county's trade inspection schedule lists a mechanical inspection at $75, and a simple trade permit can be sent by email.
  • Harnett County. Some ZIP-code listings put parts of 27592 in Harnett County. We could not confirm that from an official source. If your tax bill comes from Harnett, its Central Permitting office in Lillington takes HVAC change-out permits online.

We look up the parcel before quoting a replacement so the right office gets the permit the first time.

What heats a Willow Spring home

This is one of the most electric-heated areas we serve. Census estimates for ZIP 27592 (2024 five-year ACS) put heating fuel at about 88% electricity, 7% propane and only 4% utility gas. On the Johnston side, the county assessor lists 88% of homes with a heat pump and 11% with ducted forced air, which usually means a furnace.

The housing is a mix of rural lots and subdivisions. The median build year across the 7,152 parcels is 2004, and the busiest building decades were the 1990s (1,619 homes) and 2000s (2,139), with more than 1,300 added since 2020. About 17% of housing units in the ZIP are mobile or manufactured homes; the Johnston side alone has 124 double-wide manufactured homes on record.

What that means when you call

Heat pumps from the 1990s and 2000s build-out. A house built in 2000 is likely on its second outdoor unit by now, or overdue for one. Our one completed Willow Spring job, in October 2026, was a repair call at a 2000-built home on the Wake County side.

Propane. With gas lines scarce, propane furnaces and heat pumps with propane backup are more common here than in Garner or Fuquay-Varina proper. If you have a tank, say so when you call. A low tank or a regulator problem looks like a furnace failure.

Manufactured homes. These usually have the furnace or air handler in a hall closet and ductwork running under the floor, where crossover ducts and belly ducts can tear or come loose. Weak airflow in one end of the home often starts there.

Natural gas availability and the electric provider for Willow Spring were not confirmed in our research. Some addresses may be on Duke Energy Progress and others on an electric co-op, so check your bill before asking about a utility rebate.

Willow Spring FAQ

Who issues HVAC permits in Willow Spring?

It depends on the parcel. Most Wake County addresses go through Wake County Permits and Inspections, some through Fuquay-Varina or Angier, and Johnston County addresses go through Johnston County Building Inspections.

Do you work on propane furnaces in Willow Spring?

Yes. About 7% of homes in ZIP 27592 heat with propane, according to Census estimates. Tell us about the tank when you call.

Do you service manufactured homes?

Yes. About 17% of housing units in ZIP 27592 are mobile or manufactured homes, and we check the under-floor ductwork as well as the equipment.

How long does it take you to get to Willow Spring?

About 32 minutes and 17.5 miles from our Clayton base without traffic.
