What goes wrong in a new heat pump home

New systems don't usually wear out. They fail from how they were set up. These are the things worth checking in the first couple of years:

  • Thermostat settings. Backup heat that comes on too readily, or a heat pump setting that doesn't match the equipment, can drive up the bill without anything being broken.
  • Condensate drain. New construction leaves dust and debris in drain lines. A clogged drain or a tripped float switch shuts the system off in summer.
  • Airflow. Undersized returns, a closed damper or a filter that doesn't fit right show up as rooms that never get comfortable, especially upstairs in two-story plans.
  • Refrigerant charge. Charge that wasn't set right at startup can show up as weak cooling in the first hot summer.

If it's a warranty item, we'll tell you, and you can go back to the builder. If it isn't, we fix it.
