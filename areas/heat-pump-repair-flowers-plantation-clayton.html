Why a six-year-old heat pump stops heating

These are the checks we run on a no-heat call, in roughly the order we run them:

  1. Thermostat mode and setup. Newer smart thermostats need the right heat pump settings (reversing valve energized on cool or on heat, backup heat staging). A wrong setting can make a healthy heat pump blow cool air in heat mode.
  2. Outdoor unit iced over. Some frost on a cold, damp morning is normal and the unit should clear it in a defrost cycle. A solid block of ice means defrost isn't working: a sensor, the defrost control board or low refrigerant.
  3. Capacitor or contactor. If the outdoor fan or compressor doesn't start, these parts are the first suspects even on young equipment.
  4. Reversing valve. If the system cools fine in summer but won't switch to heat, the valve or its solenoid is the likely problem.
  5. Backup heat strips. On a cold night the heat strips in the air handler carry part of the load. If a strip or its breaker has failed, the house falls behind even though the heat pump is running.
  6. Refrigerant charge. A leak shows up as weak heat and long run times. We find the leak before adding refrigerant.

Heat pump questions from Flowers Plantation

My heat pump is blowing cool air in heat mode. What's wrong?

Check the thermostat is set to heat and isn't calling for cooling. If it is, the usual causes are a reversing valve problem, a defrost problem or failed backup heat. Call us and we'll diagnose it.

Is ice on my outdoor unit normal?

Light frost that clears during a defrost cycle is normal in cold, damp weather. A solid block of ice is not; turn the system to emergency heat and call.

My home was built in 2019. Is my heat pump still under warranty?

Often the parts warranty is still active on equipment that young. We check the model and serial before pricing a major part.

Who permits a heat pump replacement in Flowers Plantation?

Johnston County Building Inspections, because Flowers Plantation is unincorporated county land. We file the permit.
