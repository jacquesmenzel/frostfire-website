Most homes in 27545 heat with electricity

The Census Bureau's 2024 American Community Survey estimates that 68% of occupied homes in ZIP 27545 heat with electricity and about 27% with utility gas. Inside Knightdale town limits it's about 58% electric. Around here, electric heat almost always means a heat pump with backup electric heat strips in the air handler. Wake County codes the heat type as forced air, which doesn't separate gas from heat pump, so check your own equipment.

What that means in winter:

  • Frost on the outdoor unit is normal; a solid block of ice isn't. A heat pump should defrost itself every so often. If ice keeps building, the defrost control, a sensor or the refrigerant charge needs a look. Our heat pump icing guide shows the difference.
  • "Aux" or "Emergency heat" on the thermostat. Backup heat strips cost far more to run than the heat pump. If they run all the time in mild weather, something is wrong with the heat pump side.
  • Warm air, not hot. Heat pump air feels lukewarm compared to a gas furnace. That's normal as long as the house holds temperature.

How a Knightdale permit works

The Town of Knightdale Development Services issues trade permits for HVAC, electrical and plumbing, but they're submitted through the Wake County Permit Portal, and Wake County does the mechanical inspections for the town. Fees are paid through the portal or by check made out to Wake County, and the contractor has to be linked to the permit in the portal. Wake County's cutoff for a next-day inspection request is 3:00 p.m. the business day before. In practice, a heat pump swap here usually means one portal application and a county inspector at the house.

Knightdale Station questions

Is it normal for my Knightdale Station heat pump to blow lukewarm air?

Yes, if the house stays at the set temperature. Heat pump air feels cooler than gas furnace air. If the house falls behind, or the aux heat runs all the time, call us.

Why does my thermostat say Aux Heat?

Backup electric strips come on when the heat pump can't keep up or is defrosting. Short bursts are normal. Running constantly in mild weather points to a heat pump problem.

Who inspects a heat pump replacement in Knightdale Station?

The Town of Knightdale issues the permit through the Wake County Permit Portal, and Wake County performs the mechanical inspection.
