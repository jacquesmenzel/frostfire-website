We service heat pumps, AC and furnaces in Corinth-Holders. It's about 14 minutes (6.6 miles) from Clayton's town center in free-flowing traffic. Call (919) 230-4439 or request a time; same price on Sundays.

Where Corinth-Holders is, for permit purposes

Corinth-Holders isn't a town. It's an unincorporated community in Johnston County; Wikipedia lists "Corinth-Holder" as another name for Hocutts Crossroads, where NC 96 meets NC 231. There are no town limits, so the Town of Clayton doesn't permit work here even though many addresses use Clayton's 27527 ZIP code.

Which county permits your HVAC work
Johnston County side (53 of 63 homes we matched)Johnston County Building Inspections. HVAC change-outs need a permit. The county says permits can't be filed online yet; a simple trade permit can be emailed to inspections@johnstonnc.gov.
Wake County side (10 homes)Wake County Permit and Inspections trade permit, filed through Wake's online portal.

A Johnston County note: from April 1, 2026, construction drawings must be submitted digitally to the county. That's for drawings, not a simple HVAC trade permit, so a normal system replacement isn't affected. We file the permit either way.

Mostly 2010s homes, all heat pumps

The homes we matched to Corinth-Holders in county records fall into two groups: 11 built in the 1990s and 52 built in the 2010s, with a median build year of 2017. The Johnston County assessor lists every one of the 53 Johnston-side homes as a heat pump home.

That points to a clear service picture:

  • The 2010s homes have heat pumps that are roughly 7 to 15 years old if original. That's often when the first real repairs show up: capacitors, contactors, outdoor fan motors, and defrost controls.
  • The 1990s homes are a different conversation. A heat pump from the 1990s that's never been replaced is past its expected life, and if it uses R-22 refrigerant, a leak usually means it's time to replace. Many of these have already been replaced once; we'll check.
  • Backup heat. In an all-heat-pump area, the electric heat strips in the air handler matter on the coldest nights. If the house falls behind in a cold snap, we test the strips and the thermostat's backup heat settings.

The wider 27527 ZIP code is more mixed: Census estimates show about 66% of occupied homes heating with electricity and 29% with utility gas. In Corinth-Holders itself, the county data says heat pump.

Who supplies your power

This one isn't settled. Three electric co-ops have territory in Johnston County (South River EMC, Tri-County EMC and Wake Electric). We haven't confirmed whether any of them, or Duke Energy Progress, serves a given address in Corinth-Holders. It matters if you're comparing heat pump operating costs or asking about utility programs, so check your bill. Natural gas availability here also isn't confirmed.
